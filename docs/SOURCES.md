# Sources

Field Shift's product scope is Brazil nationwide. Sources with Rio Verde/GO scope below remain demonstration or local evidence only; they are not used to limit the national municipal-context boundary or to generalize a value to another location.

- IBGE Produção Agrícola Municipal, tabela SIDRA 5457: área colhida e quantidade produzida municipal do último ano publicado. Contexto estatístico somente; presença não significa aptidão. “Arroz (em casca)” e “Feijão (em grão)” não distinguem sistema ou espécie.
- Embrapa Arroz e Feijão: páginas de ZARC para feijão-comum e arroz de terras altas, usadas como referência de identidade/escopo. Nenhum parâmetro numérico foi inferido.

- NASA POWER Daily API: https://power.larc.nasa.gov/docs/services/api/temporal/daily/
- NASA POWER meteorology sources and GPM IMERG parameters: https://power.larc.nasa.gov/docs/methodology/data/sources_meteorology/
- NASA POWER hydrology sources and root-zone wetness parameters: https://power.larc.nasa.gov/docs/methodology/data/sources_hydrology/
- NASA EOSDIS GIBS SMAP L4 root-zone layer metadata: https://gibs.earthdata.nasa.gov/layer-metadata/v1.0/SMAP_L4_Analyzed_Root_Zone_Soil_Moisture.json
- NASA Worldview SMAP L4 root-zone soil moisture: https://worldview.earthdata.nasa.gov/?l=SMAP_L4_Analyzed_Root_Zone_Soil_Moisture
- NSIDC SMAP L4 SPL4SMGP Version 8 (historical numeric product; Earthdata Login required): https://nsidc.org/data/spl4smgp/versions/8
- ANA/SNIRH, *Coeficientes Técnicos de Uso da Água para a Agricultura Irrigada*: https://www.snirh.gov.br/portal/centrais-de-conteudos/central-de-publicacoes/ana_coeficientes_agricultura_irrigada_vf.pdf — Kc references are source-specific and are not irrigation prescriptions.
- FAO-56: https://www.fao.org/4/X0490E/x0490e0b.htm — Penman–Monteith ET0 (eq. 6, 7, 19, 21, 37–39) implemented as a climate indicator; ETc is not calculated.
- NASA POWER daily `PS` (surface pressure, kPa), used for ET0 and the elevation inferred from it.
- Open-Meteo Forecast API: https://open-meteo.com/en/docs — daily precipitation, provider-calculated FAO reference ET0 and temperature forecasts, requested in UTC with `models=best_match`. Forecast data are CC BY 4.0 with attribution required: https://open-meteo.com/en/license. The free endpoint is non-commercial/evaluation only under its terms: https://open-meteo.com/en/terms.
- NASA LP DAAC MOD13Q1 v061 (250 m, 16-day NDVI), read through the Microsoft Planetary Computer STAC catalog (collection `modis-13Q1-061`; STAC metadata: int16, scale 0.0001; fill −3000 and valid range −2000..10000 per the LP DAAC product guide): https://planetarycomputer.microsoft.com/dataset/modis-13Q1-061 — pre-processed offline into `data/ndvi/`. The ORNL DAAC subset service (https://modis.ornl.gov/data/modis_webservice.html) returned HTTP 502/503 and is not used.
- Local verified NASA Rio Verde fixture: `data/nasa-power-rio-verde.meta.json`.
- ISRIC SoilGrids 2.0 WCS: https://docs.isric.org/globaldata/soilgrids/ — global machine-learning spatial predictions at 250 m, six standard depth intervals, CC BY 4.0. `data/soilgrids/rio-verde-0-5cm.json` is an exact-coordinate offline extraction (0–5 cm), with Q0.05/Q0.5/Q0.95 values and WCS URLs per attribute; its hash and reproduction metadata are in the `.meta.json` sidecar. It is an estimate, not laboratory soil data, and must not be used outside its captured coordinate.
- ISRIC SoilGrids 2.0 aggregated 5 km GeoTIFFs: https://files.isric.org/soilgrids/latest/data_aggregated/5000m/ — official mean rasters used offline for the compact 0–30 cm municipal reference-point artifact; source URL and SHA-256 are retained per property/depth.
- NASA POWER Monthly Regional API: https://power.larc.nasa.gov/docs/services/api/temporal/monthly/ — regional 0.5° grid used offline for `GWETROOT` 2015–build year; the artifact retains every request URL/hash and never presents the index as volumetric field moisture.
- Ferreira; Santos (2020), *Zoneamento Agrícola de Risco Climático para Grandes Culturas no Município de Rio Verde, Goiás*: local PDF `2021-Marco Antônio Alves Ferreira-ZONEAMENTO AGRÍCOLA DE RISCO CLIMÁTICO PARA GRANDES CULTURAS NO MUNICÍPIO DE RIO VERDE, GOIÁS.pdf`, tables 1, 2, 4, 5, pp. 4–7.
- MAPA ZARC Goiás: https://www.gov.br/agricultura/pt-br/assuntos/riscos-seguro/programa-nacional-de-zoneamento-agricola-de-risco-climatico/portarias/safra-vigente/goias/goias-go
- IBGE API de Malhas, municípios do Brasil em qualidade mínima: https://servicodados.ibge.gov.br/api/v3/malhas/paises/BR?formato=application/vnd.geo+json&qualidade=minima&intrarregiao=municipio — artifact and SHA-256 in `data/brazil-municipalities.json`.
- IBGE API de Localidades, municípios: https://servicodados.ibge.gov.br/api/v1/localidades/municipios — names/UF joined by seven-digit geocode.
- MAPA ZARC Tábua de Risco 2026/2027: https://dados.agricultura.gov.br/dataset/6d3d141c-885e-41a4-ab7f-dc8ff323b96f/resource/139e5a60-1f43-4cc8-aeab-a35dbbf816c0 — P0 municipal index and source SHA-256 in `data/brazil-zarc-index.json`.
- Parameter-level value/unit/context/cultivar/location/year/URL/page/quote: `data/crop-evidence.json`. Audit: `docs/crop-evidence-report.md`.
- NASA SRTM GL1 v003 (1 arc-second): https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/data/catalog/lpcloud-srtmgl1-003 — NASA JPL (2013), doi:10.5067/MEASURES/SRTM/SRTMGL1.003; coverage 60°N–56°S, voids filled with ASTER GDEM v2. Accessed through OpenTopoData (https://www.opentopodata.org/datasets/srtm/, endpoint `https://api.opentopodata.org/v1/srtm30m`, which serves SRTM version 3).
- Embrapa relief classes: Weber, E. et al., *Indicação Geográfica Campanha Gaúcha*, Embrapa Uva e Vinho, cap. 5 (Relevo), Tabela 5.3: https://www.infoteca.cnptia.embrapa.br/infoteca/bitstream/doc/1186394/1/Cap05-IGCampanha-Weberetal.pdf
- Modified Fournier Index and its classes: Arnoldus (1980) as quoted with CEC (1992) by Khentouche, A. & Bella, N., Acta Geographica Lodziensia: https://journals.ltn.lodz.pl/Acta-Geographica-Lodziensia/article/download/2022/1789
- Crop parameters added on 2026-11-14 (each with page and verbatim excerpt in `data/crop-evidence.json`): Embrapa Milho e Sorgo, *Cultivo do Sorgo* (Clima, 6ª ed. 2010; Ecofisiologia, 9ª ed. 2015); Embrapa Milho e Sorgo, *Manejo da cultura do milho* (Circular Técnica 87, 2006); Embrapa Soja, *Tecnologias de produção de soja* (Sistemas de Produção 17, 2020); Embrapa-CNPSO, *A cultura do girassol* (Circular Técnica 13, 1996); Embrapa CPATSA, *Recomendações técnicas para o cultivo do feijoeiro comum* (1996); Embrapa Acre, *Recomendações para a cultura do feijão no Estado do Acre* (Circular Técnica 24, 1998).

## Open-Meteo — live weather (added 2026-11-14)
- Used for: current temperature, apparent temperature, humidity, rain, wind, gusts, pressure and cloud cover at the chosen point (`/api/weather`, server-side, cached 5 min; refreshed every 5 min in the page).
- Provider: https://open-meteo.com/ (CC BY 4.0, free for non-commercial use). Weather-model analysis refreshed every 15 min; **not NASA data** and not a farm measurement.
- Limits: current conditions only (no forecast), any missing field stays N/D, shown apart from NASA historical data. Must be cited in the submission references.
- Crop Kc and stage lengths (2026-11-15): Allen, R. G.; Pereira, L. S.; Raes, D.; Smith, M. (1998), *Crop evapotranspiration*, FAO Irrigation and Drainage Paper 56, Chapter 6, Table 11 (lengths of crop development stages) and Table 12 (single Kc): https://www.fao.org/4/X0490E/x0490e0b.htm. Used as a generic reference; each record names the Table 11 row. Not Brazil calibration. ETc is still not calculated. Ciclos FAO-56 (Tabela 11) não projetados por decisão do produto (15/11/2026): totais misturam climas e não representam calendários brasileiros.
- Biological nitrogen fixation, qualitative (2026-11-15): Embrapa Soja, *Inoculação e inoculante* (AIT): https://www.embrapa.br/web/agencia-de-informacao-tecnologica/cultivos/soja/producao/inoculacao-e-inoculante; Embrapa, *Feijão-Caupi: o produtor pergunta, a Embrapa responde* (2017), cap. Fixação biológica de nitrogênio: https://www.infoteca.cnptia.embrapa.br/infoteca/bitstream/doc/1085761/1/FixacaobiologicadeNitrogenio0001.pdf; Embrapa Arroz e Feijão (2011), germoplasma silvestre de feijoeiro comum: https://www.alice.cnptia.embrapa.br/alice/bitstream/doc/899486/1/3630.pdf; Embrapa Agrobiologia, Boletim PD 66 (2010), crotalária-juncea: https://www.infoteca.cnptia.embrapa.br/infoteca/bitstream/doc/884716/1/bot06610.pdf; Embrapa Agrobiologia, Comunicado Técnico 57 (2003), guandu: https://www.infoteca.cnptia.embrapa.br/bitstream/doc/624780/1/cot057.pdf.
